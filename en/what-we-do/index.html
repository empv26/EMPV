EMPV / What we do

From understanding workflows to building the systems that support them.

We analyze how work gets done, build custom software, connect existing tools and assess where AI can add value. Not everything needs to be rebuilt from scratch.

01 / Method

EMPV Discovery

We examine how a business operates, which tools it uses and where information gets lost, duplicated or slows down everyday work.

  • When it helps: Before deciding what to build or automate.
  • What we define: Problems, constraints and implementation priorities.
02 / Development

Custom software

We design applications, business platforms and management systems around an organization's workflows, responsibilities and operational needs.

  • When it helps: When existing tools cannot adequately support the work.
  • What we build: Internal applications, platforms and management systems.
03 / Integrations

Automation & integrations

We connect software and information to reduce repetitive tasks, manual data entry and handoffs between disconnected tools.

  • Where we work: Email, documents, spreadsheets and business software.
  • What we check: Available integrations, data quality and necessary controls.
04 / Applied AI

AI for business

We build AI capabilities to process documents, retrieve information and support operational tasks, with human review where needed.

  • When it helps: When traditional rules and software cannot adequately handle complex information.
  • How we work: Testing, error evaluation and supervision.
05 / Local AI

Private & on-premise AI

We design and evaluate AI solutions on controlled infrastructure when data handling and reduced cloud dependence are important requirements.

  • When it helps: When privacy, control or operational requirements call for a different deployment approach.
  • What we assess: Models, infrastructure, security, performance and costs.
