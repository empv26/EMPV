EMPV / AI ACT

Where to start

Artificial intelligence is already part of many business workflows: document management, sales, recruitment and everyday work tools.

Understanding where AI is used, the risks involved and which obligations apply to the business is the first step towards compliance.

How we work

01

Business assessment

We analyse departments, workflows and AI tools, identifying purposes, responsibilities and how data is handled.

02

Risk and obligations assessment

03

Technical and operational support

Alongside the assessment of regulatory obligations, we address the technical and organisational aspects of artificial intelligence.

We evaluate tools, integrations, infrastructure, data security and oversight systems, working with relevant internal teams and professionals.

Our support is technical and organisational; where needed, we work with relevant legal professionals on legal matters. The service does not constitute a certification of compliance.

Frequently asked questions

FAQ

Does the AI Act also apply to small and medium-sized businesses?

Yes. Obligations depend on the company's role, the AI systems it uses and their purposes, not just its size.

FAQ

If employees use ChatGPT, do we need to consider the AI Act?

Professional use of AI tools calls for an assessment of how they are used, the risks and applicable obligations.

FAQ

Do you also train employees?

Yes. We organise training based on business needs and the tools used by staff.

FAQ

Do you provide support after the initial assessment?

Yes. The service can include ongoing support, documentation updates and assessment of new tools introduced into the business.
